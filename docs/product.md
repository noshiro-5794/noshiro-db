# Product and catalogue scope

Noshiro DB brings together knowledge about works, characters, creators, and
releases across anime, manga, games, and related media. The catalogue connects
titles, editions, credits, and related entities, while keeping the source of
each supported claim visible.

## Explore the catalogue

Works have stable identifiers. A release describes a specific edition, and
episodes can carry their own airing events. Character and creator pages build
on the same entity model, so users can follow relationships across works and
providers.

Curated browsing currently focuses on anime and visual novels. The anime calendar
covers scheduled airings, while linked records extend the catalogue to other
works, people, organizations, and releases. Coverage varies by provider and by
the sources available for each entity.

## Build a personal library

A signed-in user can track status, ratings, progress, and tags, organize works
into collections, and publish reviews. Public profiles, collections, and reviews
support discovery through other users’ records. Community features include
activity, posts, reactions, bookmarks, comments, and moderation.
